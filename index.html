Security First

KAIRUS uses advanced cryptography to keep your notes safe from unauthorized access.

  • AES-256-CBC End-to-end encryption
  • PBKDF2 Key derivation (100,000 iterations)
  • Unique Salt Per encryption operation
  • Secure Folders Password-protected
  • Secure Hashing For password storage
